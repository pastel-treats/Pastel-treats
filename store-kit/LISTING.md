**Full description:**
Pastel Treats is a premium home baking house in Dindigul. Cakes, entremets, brownies and cookies are baked fresh to order in small batches by a certified pastry chef (Lavonne Academy of Baking Science & Pastry Arts), using Van Houten couverture, Callebaut chocolates and fresh buttercream.

With the app you can:
• Browse the full menu and rate card
• Customise your cake — size, flavour, message and design
• Pick your pickup date and time slot (order 48 hrs / 2 days prior)
• Pay the advance by UPI and get an order ID instantly
• Track all your orders with just your mobile number
• Get courier for brownies, blondies, cookie boxes and ragi puffs
• See the latest announcements and chat with the baker on WhatsApp

Open every day, 9 AM – 7 PM.
